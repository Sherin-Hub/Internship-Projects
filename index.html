MediBot is an intelligent AI-powered healthcare assistant designed to help users understand basic symptoms, book hospital appointments, and access emergency support — all in one place, in your preferred language.

💊

Symptom Analysis

Describe your symptoms in plain language and get basic health guidance with suggested medications and care tips.

⚡ When to call 112?

  • Unconscious or unresponsive person
  • Severe chest pain or difficulty breathing
  • Heavy bleeding or trauma injury
  • Suspected stroke (face drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty)
  • Severe allergic reaction or poisoning
